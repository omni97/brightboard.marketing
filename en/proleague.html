Frequently asked questions about advertising at Pro League clubs

How do I advertise in football stadiums?

Through BrightBoard you advertise on the LED boarding in the stadiums of Pro League clubs such as Royal Antwerp FC, Royale Union Saint-Gilloise, Sint-Truidense VV, SK Beveren, SV Zulte Waregem, K.A.A. Westerlo, Lommel SK, KRC Genk, KV Kortrijk, KV Mechelen and OH Leuven, and at clubs in the Challenger Pro League. You book advertising time per minute, per match or per season.

How does LED boarding advertising in a football stadium work?

Your ad appears on the LED boarding along the pitch during matches. That makes you visible at the crucial moments — goals, replays and decisive phases — when fans are most engaged.

Is LED boarding advertising shown on TV?

Absolutely. LED boarding is mainly aimed at viewers at home, but of course the fans in the stadium see your ad too.

How long is my ad visible during a football match?

You buy advertising time per minute. Each minute consists of 4 showings of 15 seconds on the LED boarding.

Can I book LED boarding per match or per season?

Both. You can book advertising time for a single match or for a full season.

Can I advertise at several football clubs at once?

Certainly. You can spread your campaign across several clubs at the same time.

Can I advertise at a football club in my region?

We look together at which clubs and matches best fit your region.

Which files do I need for LED boarding advertising?

Technical specifications differ from club to club; we'll send you the right ones. Don't have an animation yet? We'll gladly create one for you.

How much does LED boarding advertising in the Pro League cost?

The price depends on factors such as the club, supply and demand, and the number of minutes you book. Contact us for a tailored proposal.
