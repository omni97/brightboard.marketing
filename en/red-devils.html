What is VBR? Before and after

With VBR (Virtual Boarding Replacement), you bring your brand to the Red Devils' Belgian fans during their away matches. Partners' ads are shown specifically to the Belgian audience in the broadcast, without any loss of quality.

Enhance visibility with

Virtual overlay technology allows you to dynamically adapt the content shown on LED boarding to different audiences, moments, and platforms. By integrating virtual ads into the live broadcast feed, you can localize and personalize your messaging — without altering the in-stadium experience.

Frequently asked questions about advertising during Red Devils matches

What is VBR (Virtual Boarding Replacement) in football?

With VBR, the advertising on the LED boarding is virtually replaced in the live broadcast. The Belgian TV audience sees the partners' ads, while the experience in the stadium stays the same.

During which Red Devils matches can I advertise?

Only during the Red Devils' away matches in the Nations League. Through VBR, the Belgian audience then sees the partners' ads, without any loss of quality.

On which TV channels is advertising during Red Devils matches shown?

Your ad is shown in the broadcasts on VTM and RTL.
